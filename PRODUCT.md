# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners looking to establish an online presence for their small business. They need a professional website to showcase their services, manage content, and attract customers.

## Product Purpose

A reusable Astro template for small business websites. Enables快速 deployment of professional, content-managed sites with Storyblok CMS integration.

## Positioning

A ready-made, deployable template that combines modern web tech (Astro, Tailwind) with headless CMS capabilities, specifically designed for small business needs.

## Operating Context

- Content managed via Storyblok CMS (visual editor, content preview)
- Deployed on Vercel with server/static rendering modes
- SEO-optimized with sitemap generation
- Analytics via Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Storyblok-powered content management
- Responsive design with Tailwind CSS
- Component-based architecture (hero, features, FAQ, testimonials, galleries, contact forms)
- Sitemap excludes opt-out pages
- Image optimization via Storyblok
- Must preserve existing URL structure and SEO authority
